What our vans carry

Every TyresVision mobile van is a heavy-duty, self-contained workshop on wheels — not a handyman with a spare tyre and a hand jack. We carry professional workshop equipment engineered to protect your alloy wheels and ensure factory-grade safety.

Tyre changer

Electro-pneumatic tyre changers with scratch-free nylon clamp protectors, capable of safely mounting low-profile tyres and stiff run-flat beads up to 24-inch alloys without marking the rim finish.

On-site wheel balancer

High-precision mobile wheel balancing in Dubai and Abu Dhabi performed on-site at your vehicle for every tyre change. We never skip balancing or ask you to visit a garage afterwards.

Calibrated torque wrench

Digital and micrometer torque wrenches calibrated to your car's exact manufacturer Newton-metre (Nm) specification. We never rely on overtightened impact guns or loose hand wrenches.

New valves, every fitting

A degraded rubber valve stem is the leading cause of slow pressure leaks in high UAE heat. We replace standard valves with fresh premium stems on every fitting, and inspect TPMS seals.

Space for your old tyres

Our vans have dedicated cargo racks to collect and transport your worn rubber to licensed recycling facilities in the UAE. You don't have to haul dirty tyres in your boot or find storage.

Where we can and can't fit

Safety and customer transparency come first. We would much rather advise you honestly upfront than dispatch a commercial van that cannot complete the job safely on-site:

Yes
Villa driveway or private compound — Our most common job. Plenty of level room for hydraulic jacks, vehicle positioning, and rapid doorstep tyre replacement.
Yes
Apartment & office car parks — Easily handled provided building security grants van entrance and visitor clearance is registered in advance so our technicians can park beside you.
Ask first
Height-restricted basements — Our mobile tyre vans require a minimum ceiling clearance of 2.2m. Check your tower's height barrier with us on WhatsApp before booking.
No
Busy highway shoulders (e.g., Sheikh Zayed Road) — Extremely unsafe for on-road tyre mounting. For live high-speed motorways, we arrange flatbed vehicle recovery to a partner workshop instead.

EVs, run-flats and 4x4s at your location

Electric vehicles must be lifted strictly on designated chassis jacking pads — never on the high-voltage battery casing — and our mobile vans carry manufacturer-approved rubber pucks for Tesla, BYD, and premium EVs. We also carry reinforced bead-press equipment engineered for stiff run-flat sidewalls and high-clearance 4x4 all-terrain tyres.
