How mobile tyre fitting works

Booking a mobile tyre service in Dubai or Abu Dhabi is one continuous handoff, from your phone to your driveway.

1. Send your tyre size

Snap the sidewall or tell us your car make and model on WhatsApp.

2. Drop a location pin

Home, office or apartment car park — the van comes to the exact spot (not just the area).

3. We confirm a time

Agreed on WhatsApp before the van leaves the depot, so you're not waiting all day.

4. Fitted where you're parked

New tyres mounted, balanced and torqued to spec — no drop-off, no second trip.

What our vans carry

Each van is a self-contained workshop, not a spare tyre and a jack.

Tyre changer

Full mounting and demounting equipment for run-flats and low-profile tyres.

On-site wheel balancer

Balances happen at your location, every time — it's never quickly skipped.

Calibrated torque wrench

Wheel nuts torqued to the manufacturer's exact spec, not just tightened by hand.

New valves, every fitting

Space for your old tyres

Where we can and can't fit

We'd rather tell you upfront than send a van that can't do the job:

Yes
Villa driveway — our most common job, plenty of room to work safely.
Yes

Height-restricted basements/parking — some of our vans won't clear the entrance, so check with us before booking.
No
Busy roadside on Sheikh Zayed Road or similar highways — too dangerous to fit safely. We'll arrange recovery to a partner centre instead.

EVs, run-flats and 4x4s at your location

Electric vehicles must be lifted on their marked jacking pads only — never on the battery housing — and our vans carry specific mounting equipment for run-flats and heavy 4x4s. Learn more on our dedicated EV and run-flat tyres page, or verify your vehicle dimensions to find your tyre size.
