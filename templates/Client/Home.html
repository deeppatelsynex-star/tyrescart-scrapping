Frequently asked questions

Got questions? We’ve got answers.

How do I find my tyre size?

Your tyre size is printed on the sidewall of your current tyres. It looks like 235/55 R19 105W (Width / Profile / Rim / Load & Speed). You can also find it on the sticker inside your driver’s door frame, or in your vehicle manual. If you’re unsure, just send us a photo of your tyre on WhatsApp and we’ll identify it for you.

Is fitting included in the price?

Yes. Delivery to your chosen fitting centre is free and fitting is arranged for you. For mobile fitting at your home or office, a small call-out fee may apply depending on your location, which we quote upfront on WhatsApp before you commit.

Are your tyres date-fresh and genuine?

Every tyre sold through TyresVision is 100% genuine, brand-new, and sourced through authorised UAE distributors. We only sell tyres with fresh DOT production dates conforming to UAE / GCC standards, and eligible tyres carry manufacturer warranty.

How fast can I get my tyres fitted?

Same-day and next-day fitting slots are available across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and Ajman for most popular sizes. If your size is specialized or imported, we’ll give you an exact timeline on WhatsApp.

How do I pay?

You can pay securely via credit/debit card, bank transfer, or cash on completion depending on the fitting arrangement chosen.

Do you offer fleet services for businesses?

Yes. We provide consolidated billing, fleet tyre inspections, scheduled on-site mobile fitting, and volume discounts for corporate fleets across the UAE. Call us on +971 50 506 9575 to discuss fleet accounts.
