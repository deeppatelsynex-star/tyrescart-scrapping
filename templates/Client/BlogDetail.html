Understanding Your Tyre Options in the UAE

When selecting tyres for UAE driving, temperature rating, treadwear grade, and traction rating are paramount. Extreme asphalt temperatures during Gulf summers can cause rapid pressure increases and accelerated compound degradation.

Standard Compressed Air vs. Nitrogen Inflation

Maintaining consistent tyre pressure improves handling, extends tyre lifespan, and optimises fuel economy across long highway journeys.

Feature Standard Compressed Air Purified Nitrogen (N2) UAE Road Advantage
Pressure Stability Fluctuates with heat Maintains steady pressure Essential for Gulf summer heat
Moisture Content Contains water vapor Dry & moisture-free Protects internal tyre cords
Leakage Rate ~1–2 PSI per month ~0.3–0.5 PSI per month Fewer top-ups needed
Availability Every petrol station Tyre centres & mobile vans Available across all TyresVision fitters

Key Maintenance Recommendations

  1. Check Cold Pressures: Measure tyre pressure in the morning before driving or after the car has rested for 3+ hours.
  2. Inspect Tread Depth: Ensure minimum 3.0mm tread depth prior to long highway runs between Dubai and Abu Dhabi.
  3. Rotate Every 10,000 KM: Front-to-rear rotation ensures even wear and maximizes mileage warranty.
